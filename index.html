Roblox Shorts Uploader

Roblox Shorts Uploader is a small internal tool used by a team of independent creators. It uploads short gaming videos about Roblox that we produce ourselves to our own YouTube channel.

The tool uses the YouTube API Services only to upload videos after a team member has reviewed and approved each one. It does not access, collect or store data from any other YouTube users or channels.
